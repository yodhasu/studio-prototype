Redefining the Center Metric

The circular gauge currently at 84% should represent something valuable for a solo artist, not just enterprise managers.

A

Creative Flow / Focus

Tracks your "Deep Work" hours for the day. 100% means you've hit your flow target.

B

Weekly Momentum

A rolling average of your activity. Keeps you motivated to stay consistent without the pressure of a deadline.

C

Project Progression

Total completion percentage across all active pieces. Great for seeing the finish line of a collection.

D

Personal Goal

A custom goal you set (e.g., "5 Sketches this week"). Tracks your progress toward that specific milestone.

Which feels most useful for your daily creative routine?
